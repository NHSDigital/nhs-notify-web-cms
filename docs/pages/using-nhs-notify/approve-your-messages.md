Before you send messages to recipients, you must review and approve your templates. This allows you to check how your messages will be displayed. This is sometimes referred to as proofing.

It’s your responsibility to review your templates in a way that meets your service’s needs. Make sure:

- relevant stakeholders in your team have approved your template
- there are no errors in your formatting, personalisation or message content
- you check your message length
